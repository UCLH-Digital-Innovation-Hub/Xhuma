What Xhuma does

A bridge between NHS primary care and your record system

Xhuma sits between your EHR's document-exchange layer and NHS services, translating between standards so patient records flow without bespoke integration.

⚡

GP Connect integration

Retrieves structured patient records from NHS primary care via GP Connect, authenticated against NHS Spine.

📄

Standards-based conversion

Transforms FHIR structured records into Consolidated CDA (CCDA), implementing IHE ITI profiles your EHR already speaks.

🔒

Secure by design

Mutual TLS on every hop, full audit logging, and data handling built for personal health information.

How it works

From EHR query to NHS record in three steps

No new clinician workflow — the record arrives through the document-exchange your EHR already uses.

The EHR requests records

A clinician's system queries for a patient via IHE / Care Everywhere, as it would any other community.

Xhuma fetches and converts

Xhuma queries NHS GP Connect, resolves the patient via PDS/SDS, and converts the structured record to CCDA.

The record is returned

A standards-based CCDA document is delivered back to the EHR, ready to view in the patient's chart.

Built on open standards

GP Connect IHE XCA (ITI-38 / ITI-39) PDS SDS Epic Care Everywhere HL7 CDA / CCDA
